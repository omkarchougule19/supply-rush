## 1. Game Setup & Phases
* **Starting Cash:** $3,000,000
* **Total Quarters:** 16 (default)
* **Quarter 0 (Setup Phase):** 
  - Time for initial planning and placing warehouses.
  - Zero active demand is simulated and no delivery animations play.
  - **Golden Circles (Revealed Zones):** Dash outline markers indicating possible demand zones are visible ONLY in Quarter 0 to assist with initial placement. They do not appear during active play quarters (Q1+).

## 2. Warehouses
Warehouses have different costs, capacities, build times, and sell-back values:

| Type | Purchase Cost | Capacity (Orders/Qtr) | Build Time | Sell-Back Value |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Small** | $250,000 | 500 | 1 Quarter | $125,000 |
| **Medium** | $500,000 | 1,200 | 2 Quarters | $250,000 |
| **Large** | $800,000 | 2,500 | 4 Quarters | $400,000 |

* **Build Time Rules:** A warehouse built at quarter `B` becomes active and usable at the start of quarter `B + build_time`.
* **Fleet Capacity Cap:** A warehouse's total fleet capacity (sum of all vehicles parked at it) cannot exceed the warehouse's own capacity rating.

## 3. Fleet Vehicles
Vehicles handle deliveries from warehouses. Drones are the only vehicles that can deliver urgent orders.

| Type | Purchase Cost | Capacity (Orders/Qtr) | Operating Cost/Qtr | Sell-Back Value | Serves Urgent | Serves Non-Urgent |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Truck** | $20,000 | 200 | $800 | $12,000 | No | Yes |
| **Drone** | $9,000 | 60 | $800 | $5,400 | Yes | Yes |

* **Shared/Flexible Drone Capacity:** Drones serve both urgent and non-urgent orders. Their capacity is shared, not double-counted (e.g. a single drone can deliver 40 urgent and 20 non-urgent orders in a quarter, but not 60 of each).

## 4. Revenues & Third-Party Logistics (3PL) Outsourcing
* **Fulfillment Revenues:**
  - **Urgent Order:** $55 per order delivered
  - **Non-Urgent Order:** $25 per order delivered
* **Outsourcing Option (3PL):**
  - Allowed in all modes including the default normal game.
  - Players can toggle individual demand zones to be outsourced.
  - **Outsource Costs:** $75 per urgent order, $40 per non-urgent order.
  - **Outsource Deliveries:** Outsourced orders are 100% fulfilled instantly, completely bypassing warehouse capacity limits and fleet operating expenses.

## 5. Service & Delivery Routing
* **Service Radius:** A warehouse can only serve demand zones located within its service radius (default: 30 units distance, calculated using Euclidean formula).
* **Fulfillment Allocation:** 
  1. Zones are assigned to the nearest active warehouse within the service radius.
  2. Urgent demand is fulfilled first by drone capacity.
  3. Non-urgent demand is fulfilled by remaining drone capacity and truck capacity.
  4. Fulfillments are capped by the warehouse's overall throughput capacity and its parked vehicles' capacities.

## 6. Demand Generation & Evolution
* **Active Zone Scaling:** The number of active demand zones scales linearly from 50% of eligible zones in Quarter 1 to 100% by the second-to-last quarter.
* **Eligible Pools:** 50% of the game's selected zones are revealed at Q0. The other 50% unlock progressively during gameplay (starting at Q6).
* **Demand Evolution (Active Zones):**
  - **Growth (85% probability):** Demand grows by 5% to 20% compared to the previous quarter.
  - **Drop (15% probability):** Demand drops by 50% to 80% compared to the previous quarter.
  - Clamped between a minimum of 10 and a configured maximum (default: 800) orders.

### Bug Fix: Restricting Golden Circles (Revealed Zones) to Setup Phase (Q0)
* **Idea:** Golden circles (revealed zone outline markers) are intended only for initial planning in Quarter 0 (Setup Phase) to assist with warehouse placement. However, they were still rendering in subsequent play quarters (Q1+) if outsourcing was enabled because of an overly broad condition. We restrict the rendering of revealed zones strictly to Quarter 0.
* **Working Steps:**
  1. **Frontend:** Update the condition in `renderDemandSpots()` in [game.html](static/game.html) from `if (state.currentQuarter === 0 || (state.scenario && state.scenario.allow_outsourcing))` to strictly `if (state.currentQuarter === 0)`.
  2. **Verification:** Confirm that golden circles appear in Quarter 0 and disappear completely from Quarter 1 onward.

### Bug Fix: Restoring Drone and Truck Animations in Offline/Demo Mode
* **Idea:** The truck and drone delivery animations were failing to play because the computed animations list was empty. This occurred because `fakeDemand` (client-side demo mode fallback) generated demand spots without a `type` property (either `"urgent"` or `"nonurgent"`). As a result, the delivery animation planner could not match vehicles to targets. We fix this by explicitly assigning the `type` property when mapping spots in `fakeDemand`, and additionally adding a defensive fallback in `computeZoneAssignments` to ensure all spots have their types set before calculations.
* **Working Steps:**
  1. **Frontend:** Update `makeSpot` and maps in `fakeDemand` in [game.html](static/game.html) to assign the `type` parameter.
  2. **Frontend:** Add defensive type assignment inside `computeZoneAssignments` in [game.html](static/game.html) so all processed spots are guaranteed to have their correct `type` properties.
  3. **Verification:** Confirm that trucks and drones animate correctly from active warehouses to their served demand zones during quarter runs.

### Bug Fix: Enabling and Syncing Outsourcing (3PL) Toggle
* **Idea:** Restore the usability of the outsourcing toggle in the zone panel and prevent desyncs during quarter progressions.
  1. **Visual Switch:** The `.toggle` CSS rules were missing in [game.html](static/game.html), making the switch invisible and unclickable. We add these rules to the styles section.
  2. **Quarter Progression Sync:** When the simulation runs, the database clears outsourced zones for the next quarter, but the frontend was not resetting `state.outsourcedZones`. We clear `state.outsourcedZones` on the client when entering a new quarter so the UI does not show stale selections.
* **Working Steps:**
  1. **Frontend:** Add `.toggle` and `.toggle.on` styling rules (including the slide knob layout) to the CSS section in [game.html](static/game.html).
  2. **Frontend:** Add `state.outsourcedZones = [];` inside the client-side `done(result)` function in [game.html](static/game.html) to reset outsourcing choices at the start of each new quarter.
  3. **Verification:** Verify that the outsourcing toggle renders as a clean switch, correctly marks zones as outsourced on toggle, resolves orders via outsourcing, and resets on quarter advancement.

### Feature: Permanent Active Zones, Persistent Outsourcing, and Outsourced Countdown Animation
* **Idea:**
  1. **Permanent Active Zones:** Once a demand zone becomes active, it should stay active throughout the game. We implement this in the client-side `fakeDemand` (demo mode fallback) by carrying forward previously active zones and evolving their demand, matching the backend's behavior.
  2. **Persistent Outsourcing:** Outsourced zones should stay outsourced across quarters until explicitly toggled off by the player. We remove the reset logic from both the backend (`routes.py`) and the frontend (`game.html` `done()`).
  3. **Outsourced Zones Countdown:** During delivery animations, outsourced zones should visually count down their orders to `0` matching the active normal zones to provide a cohesive visual experience.
* **Working Steps:**
  1. **Backend:** Remove `play.outsourced_zones = json.dumps([])` in the `/advance` endpoint of [routes.py](routes.py).
  2. **Frontend:** Remove `state.outsourcedZones = [];` from `done(result)` in [game.html](static/game.html).
  3. **Frontend:** Update `fakeDemand()` in [game.html](static/game.html) to carry forward previous urgent/nonurgent active spots and evolve their order count.
  4. **Frontend:** Update `showDeliveryOverlay` in [game.html](static/game.html) to locate outsourced zones and animate their labels counting down to 0 during the delivery phase.
